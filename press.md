# **Press Kit**

Logos, colours, images and a short description for anyone writing about, reviewing or featuring MustardOS. Everything
on this page can be used to refer to the project.

[Download the Press Kit (ZIP)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/press/mustardos-press-kit.zip){: .btn .btn-primary }

## Name

Use **MustardOS** as the name of the project, written as one word with a capital M and a capital OS. The short form
**muOS** may be used where a shorter name suits the layout or style, such as a logo lock-up, a tight heading or a
social media handle.

<p class="note">Please avoid "Mustard OS", "MUOS" or "Muos". The full name is preferred whenever there is room for it.</p>

## Description

MustardOS is custom firmware (CFW) for handheld gaming devices. It is configurable, themeable and easy to use, so you
can start playing your favourite games with very little setup.

## Logos

The swirl badge is the preferred logo for MustardOS. Use it wherever one logo represents the project, such as an
article image, a store listing or an avatar. The wordmark and the M glyph are alternatives for layouts where the badge
does not fit, such as a wide banner or a small inline mark.
