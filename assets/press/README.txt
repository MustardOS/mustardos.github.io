MustardOS Press Kit
https://muos.dev/press

Name
  Use "MustardOS" as the name of the project, written as one word with a capital M and a capital OS.
  "muOS" may be used where a shorter form suits the layout or style, such as a logo lock-up, a tight
  heading or a social media handle. Please avoid "Mustard OS", "MUOS" or "Muos".

Description
  MustardOS is custom firmware (CFW) for handheld gaming devices. It is configurable, themeable and
  easy to use, so you can start playing your favourite games with very little setup.

Preferred logo
  The swirl badge (badge/) is the preferred logo for MustardOS. Use it wherever one logo represents
  the project, such as an article image, a store listing or an avatar. The wordmark and the M glyph
  are alternatives for layouts where the badge does not fit, such as a wide banner or a small mark.

Files
  badge/   The swirl badge, the preferred logo, as SVG, as transparent PNG, and on its black background
  logo/    The MustardOS wordmark in colour, white and black, as SVG and 2000 px PNG

Use

  or add effects. Leave clear space around a logo of at least the height of the M's horizontal bar.
