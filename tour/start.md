# <strong>The Grand Tour</strong>

Your guide to every screen, menu and setting in the current MustardOS release, from the main menu to advanced
settings and system information (_and maybe a secret or two_). Each stop explains what every option does, shows the
icon you will see on your device, and adds a few tips worth knowing.

    <h2 class="guide-heading">Good to know</h2>

    <div class="guide-tips">
        <div class="guide-tip">
            <img src="/tour/assets/glyphs/footer/lr.svg" alt="" width="32" height="32">
            <div>
                <span class="guide-title">Sections</span>
                <p>Busier screens such as Customisation, Storage and Pickles Settings are split into sections. Press
                    <strong>Left</strong> or <strong>Right</strong>, or use the shoulder buttons, to move between them.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="guide-tip">
            <img src="/tour/assets/glyphs/footer/x.svg" alt="" width="32" height="32">
            <div>
                <span class="guide-title">A tour on your device</span>
                <p>The first time you open a screen, a short explanation of it appears. Press <strong>X</strong> on any
                    of them to stop the tour and find your own way.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="guide-tip">
            <img src="/tour/assets/glyphs/footer/menu.svg" alt="" width="32" height="32">
            <div>
                <span class="guide-title">Help anywhere</span>
                <p>Press <strong>MENU</strong> for help with whatever you are looking at. On many screens it also lists
                    extra things you can do from there.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
